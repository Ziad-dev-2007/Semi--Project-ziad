About us

A small team that loves the dark.

We started with a bad photo of the Milky Way

Three friends from Cairo drove to Siwa to photograph the stars and came back with a blurry picture and a sunburn. A Bedouin guide named Salem showed us where to actually look. That night changed how we travel.

Now we run the same trip for small groups. We work with local families in every oasis, pay fair wages, and take everything we bring back out with us.
